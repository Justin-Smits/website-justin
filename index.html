Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked

How does the WhatsApp process work?
+
You send a WhatsApp message answering 3 short questions and attach at least 3 photos. Based on that, I build a first relevant preview within 48 hours, which we then refine together.
How long does it take to build my website?
+
You'll see a first preview within 48 hours of your WhatsApp message. The full, polished and launched site typically takes 1–3 weeks depending on complexity and how quickly you give feedback.
Is there a setup fee or upfront cost?
+
No setup fee on the monthly plans. Your first monthly payment covers the design and build of your website. Everything is included in the flat monthly rate.
What if I want to cancel?
+
All monthly plans (Starter, Growth, Pro) have a minimum term of 1 year. After that, you can cancel with 1 month's notice. You keep all your content and can export everything.
What counts as a "content change"?
+
Text updates, image swaps, adding a product, changing prices, updating contact info — anything that takes under 30 minutes. Larger structural changes (new pages, new features) are discussed separately.
Do you build webshops and e-commerce sites?
+
Yes — e-commerce is available on the Pro plan, using Shopify or WooCommerce depending on your needs. I'll recommend the right platform based on your WhatsApp answers.
I'm not technical at all — is that a problem?
+
That's exactly why you're hiring me. I handle all the technical complexity and explain everything in plain language via WhatsApp. You focus on your business — I handle the website.
What's included in the one-time €900 purchase?
+
A fully built custom website for a one-off payment — no subscription. It excludes ongoing hosting, support, and SEO/GEO optimisation, which can be added separately if you want them.
