What exactly is free?

The 20-minute online introduction is free. A morning or afternoon of process analysis is also free, either online or in person at your office. The subsequent report costs €1,500 excl. VAT and is prepared only with your approval.

Is implementation included in the report?

No. The report provides findings, action items and proposals. We separately agree the scope, timeline and costs for building and implementing the automations.

Do I need Microsoft licences already?

Not for the introduction. During the analysis, we discuss your current environment. Required licences and integrations depend on the solution and are agreed before implementation.
